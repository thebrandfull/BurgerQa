How Rankings Work

📊

Wilson Score

We use the Wilson score lower bound to calculate confidence intervals. This prevents new restaurants with few votes from dominating the rankings.

weight = 60%
⚔️

Pairwise Wins

Head-to-head comparisons using Elo-style ratings. Great for quickly separating the best from the rest.

weight = 25%
⏰

Recency Factor

Recent votes matter more. We apply a decay factor with a 6-month half-life to keep rankings fresh and relevant.

decay = 0.5^(days/180)
🛡️

Anti-Gaming

Rate limits, device fingerprinting, and anomaly detection prevent vote manipulation and ensure fair rankings.

max 20 votes/day

Final Score Formula

score = 100 × (
  0.60 × wilson_score
  + 0.25 × pairwise_rating
  + 0.15 × expert_rating
) × recency_factor × trust_weight

Minimum 30 votes required to appear in rankings. Rankings updated every 6 hours.
